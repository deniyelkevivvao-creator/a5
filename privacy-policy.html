Institutional Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL

1. Regulatory Scope and Principles

At Cycling Raincoat Atelier, accessible via cyclingraincoat.com, safeguarding the confidentiality, privacy, and integrity of all client records is our highest corporate priority. We maintain strict physical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal identity and measurements against unauthorized access, compromise, loss, or alteration. All electronic communications, bespoke appointments, and transactional records are encrypted using modern industrial security standards. We do not sell or lease your personal contact information to outside advertising syndicates under any circumstance whatsoever at any time.

2. Transparent Information Collection

When scheduling custom outerwear consultations, ordering bespoke cycling apparel, or communicating with our engineering team, we collect necessary contact information including your full name, email address, and direct telephone number. This information allows our staff to coordinate consultations, fulfill requested commissions, and maintain transparent service documentation. Furthermore, our web servers automatically log standard network telemetry including internet protocol addresses, browser types, and timestamped page visits to ensure optimal platform performance and security across all user browsing sessions.

3. Technical Cookies and Telemetry

Our digital infrastructure employs strictly essential session cookies to facilitate rapid navigation, remember user accessibility preferences, and ensure seamless appointment form submission. These technical cookies do not harvest external tracking records or monitor your personal browsing habits beyond our primary domain. Users may adjust their private browser preferences to disable non-essential cookie storage at any time without compromising essential navigation functions on this website or accessing historical archives.

4. Secure Record Retention

We store client biometric fit records, pattern measurements, and consultation histories exclusively for durations necessary to fulfill explicit business purposes, complete active commissions, and satisfy mandatory legal documentation rules. Once our service relationship concludes and statutory storage periods lapse, your identifiable records are permanently erased from our secure cloud infrastructure. You maintain full statutory rights to request an audited extract of all personal data held in our systems at any moment in time.

5. Regulatory Amendments

We reserve the unilateral operational authority to update this institutional privacy policy whenever statutory frameworks change or our internal technical systems undergo modernization. Any material modifications will be promptly published on this dedicated URL with an updated legal revision date. Continued engagement with our digital services following published revisions constitutes formal acceptance of our modernized administrative protocols, ethical standards, and client protection measures across our entire atelier.

6. Institutional Privacy Desk

If you require legal clarification regarding our privacy protocols, wish to exercise statutory data rights, or need assistance regarding your records, please contact our privacy compliance team directly. You may reach us by writing to our headquarters at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by placing a telephone call to our verified office line at +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours for immediate assistance.
