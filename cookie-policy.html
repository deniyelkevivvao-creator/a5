Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified Document ID: 181-MERCER-LEGAL

1. Cookie Governance and Telemetry Overview

This Cookie Policy elucidates how Cycling Raincoat utilizes essential digital cookies and telemetry identifiers to deliver an optimal, secure browsing experience across cyclingraincoat.com. By accessing our platform, you acknowledge our structured use of necessary session cookies engineered to facilitate rapid page rendering, protect client consultation forms, and remember personal navigation preferences between atelier visits and bespoke appointment scheduling across all modern desktop and mobile platforms.

2. Essential Functional and Session Tokens

Technical session cookies are indispensable to the core functionality of our luxury portal, allowing our servers to maintain your active salon booking sessions and preserve expanded FAQ segments. These cookies expire automatically upon closing your browser window and do not harvest personal identity files or track your online movements across third-party websites outside our secure digital perimeter under any circumstance or operating condition whatsoever.

3. Performance Analytics and Security Signals

We deploy privacy-compliant analytics identifiers, including Google Analytics Tag G-0LY0HY7L01, to monitor aggregate visitor traffic patterns, average treatise reading duration, and geographic platform demand. All telemetry data is stripped of personally identifiable identifiers, anonymizing internet protocol addresses prior to statistical aggregation so that individual client habits cannot be deciphered, cataloged, or linked to personal identities in any manner by third parties.

4. Client Preferences and Cookie Control

Functional personalization cookies allow our web servers to remember your selected preferences between salon visits, including currency selections, language regionalizations, and previously expanded accordion segments on our FAQ modules. By preserving these customized states, our digital salon delivers an efficient, uninterrupted luxury shopping experience tailored to your specific desktop or mobile device configuration across multiple browsing sessions throughout the entire operating year.

5. Policy Revisions and Regulatory Standards

Clients maintain absolute autonomous authority to inspect, accept, decline, or systematically delete digital cookies through standard configuration menus built into all contemporary web browsers including Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. Please understand that disabling certain functional or session cookies may limit your ability to view interactive outerwear galleries, access private salon booking tools, or store customized wishlist selections across our domain without interruptions.
