Commission Protocol

The Bespoke Fitting Process

Four meticulous milestones ensure an uncompromising fit and lifetime water resistance.

PHASE 01

Kinetic Scanning

3D laser measurement of riding geometry, arm reach, and neck angle on your bicycle.

PHASE 02

Membrane Selection

Calibrating ePTFE or polyurethane membranes to your target rainfall rate and climate.

PHASE 03

Ultrasonic Welded Build

High-frequency seam welding, micro-taping, and YKK AquaGuard zipper alignment.

PHASE 04

Flume Calibration

Simulated wind-tunnel rain immersion testing to verify zero water ingress before delivery.
